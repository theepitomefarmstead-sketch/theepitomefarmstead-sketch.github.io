Integrated by design

Many parts. One living system.

The farm vision explores connected cycles that make better use of resources and support healthy production. These are building blocks of an evolving project, not a claim that every system is already in place.

01

Soil & crops

Crop production, composting, and vermicomposting help build a foundation for soil regeneration.

02

Integrated livestock

Poultry and on-farm feed systems can connect animal care with the wider farm cycle.

03

Circular resources

Azolla, black soldier fly larvae, and compost are among the resource loops the project explores.

04

Local food & livelihoods

CSA, direct sales, and value-added products offer pathways to connect farm production with people.

A digital platform for a real-world farm

Better records. More informed decisions.

Our digital platform is intended to help organize farm operations and make it easier to learn from them. It will develop alongside the farm and its validated workflows—supporting useful records without trying to automate everything at once.

  • Track crops, livestock, inputs, and harvests
  • Organize purchases, sales, and inventory
  • Coordinate tasks and farm activities
  • Keep links to documents and farm records
  • Record soil, water, and ecological observations
  • Review clear operational summaries
