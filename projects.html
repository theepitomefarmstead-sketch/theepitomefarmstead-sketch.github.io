The project

A living farm, built together.

The campaign image describes a community effort to create a regenerative farm: a place to grow organic food, care for the land, conserve water, and learn by doing. The farm is envisioned as an interconnected landscape rather than a collection of separate enterprises.

Its proposed building blocks include:

  • Food forest
  • Water systems
  • Integrated livestock
  • Organic gardens
  • Compost and vermicompost
  • Azolla and feed system
  • Plant nursery
  • Farm infrastructure
