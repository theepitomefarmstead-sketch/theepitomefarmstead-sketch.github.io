# Mission

The Epitome Integrated Organic Farm exists to model a regenerative, local, and community-centered approach to food production and land stewardship in the Kathmandu Valley.

## Purpose

- Grow healthy food through regenerative and integrated farming practices.
- Restore soil, biodiversity, and ecological resilience over time.
- Build a farm that is both productive and deeply rooted in place.
- Support a stronger local food system through education, access, and participation.

## Target audience

- Course students learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents.
- Developers giving AI coding demonstrations at conference booths.

## Core values

- Regeneration over extraction
- Practical learning over perfection
- Community over isolation
- Transparency over hype
- Long-term stewardship over short-term gain

## What we are building

We are creating a farm identity and digital presence that communicates the vision clearly: a living farm that connects ecological health, social wellbeing, and viable local livelihoods.

## Non-goals

- Building a large-scale industrial operation before the model is proven.
- Over-automating the farm before workflows are tested on the ground.
- Presenting claims of full sustainability without evidence and lived learning.
